User Manual

For Analysts & Principal Fund Managers
Internal analytical tool — not investment advice. Document version 1.1 · July 2026
Who this is for. This manual covers day-to-day use of the BRB NGX Analyst platform for two roles: the Analyst (research, screening, portfolio construction and management, AI-assisted review) and the Principal Fund Manager (PFM) (everything an analyst can do, plus reviewing and approving rebalancing proposals). Administrator-only functions (user & API-key management) are summarised at the end. A separate Deployment Guide exists for the engineering team.

1 · Getting started & roles

Open the platform URL provided by your administrator and sign in with your work email and password. If you do not have an account, ask an administrator to create one for you.

RoleWhat it can do
Analyst Research the market, screen equities, build and manage model portfolios, run AI reviews, set alerts, export data. Sees and owns its own portfolios.
PFM Everything an analyst can do, plus the Approvals queue: review, approve or reject rebalancing proposals submitted by analysts.
Admin Everything, plus user management, API-key configuration, quota & audit dashboards. Sees all analysts' portfolios.
First login. Change your password is managed by an administrator. If your firm is still using the seeded demo accounts (analyst@brb.local, pm@brb.local, admin@brb.local), ask engineering to rotate them before real use.

2 · Navigating the platform

The top navigation bar gives you: Dashboard, Stocks, Screener, Portfolios, Watchlists, Instruments and Forecasting. On the right you'll find the notification bell, a light/dark theme toggle, and your user menu (name, role, sign out). PFMs also see an approvals icon; admins see a settings gear.

The platform opens in dark mode by default; use the sun/moon toggle to switch. Your choice is remembered.

3 · Market Dashboard

The landing page is your NGX daily overview. It has two tabs: Market Overview and AI Daily Summary.

Market Overview shows:

  • Market status banner — open/closed and next session time (Africa/Lagos).
  • All-Share Index hero — level, day change, YTD, deals, volume, value traded, total market cap, and breadth (advancers / decliners / unchanged).
  • Indices strip — NGX30, NGX50, sector and thematic indices.
  • Most Active (by naira value traded) and FX (naira exchange rates).
  • Movers, YTD performers, and Sector performance.
Derived data. Where your data plan does not include an aggregate directly (e.g. sector performance or YTD leaders), the platform reconstructs it exactly from the underlying constituent list and labels it "derived from constituents (equal-weighted)". These are real calculations, not estimates.

All prices carry an "as of" timestamp and are delayed up to 20 minutes during NGX hours (last close otherwise).

4 · AI Daily Market Summary

On the Dashboard, open the AI Daily Summary tab and click Generate today's summary. Claude reads the day's snapshot, movers, sectors and FX and writes a desk-style briefing (headline, market action, movers & sectors, FX, takeaway). One summary is kept per trading day; use Regenerate to refresh it.

  • Include web context (cited) — tick this to let Claude run up to three web searches for qualitative context (policy, corporate events). Sources are cited; NGX figures always come from the platform's own data, never the web.
Compliance. The summary is descriptive market commentary for internal use — not investment advice, a recommendation, or a prediction. Verify figures against the dashboard before relying on them.

5 · Stock Analysis & AI filing extraction

Go to Stocks, search a ticker or company name, and open its analysis page. Tabs cover the quote and chart, fundamentals and headline ratios, peers, dividends, and news.

AI statement extraction

On the Fundamentals tab, the AI statement extraction panel lists the company's official NGX "Financial Statements" filings. Click Extract with Claude and the platform downloads the official filing PDF and extracts the income statement, balance sheet and cash flow as clean tables, with page-level citations back to the source document. Each extraction is cached; use Re-extract to refresh.

Always traceable. Every extracted figure carries a [p.N] marker pointing at the page of the filing, and the source PDF is one click away. Verify against the PDF before relying on the numbers.

6 · Screener

Under Screener, filter the NGX universe by search term, sector and market-cap band, and sort by any column (price, day, 1-month, YTD, 52-week). Useful workflows:

  • Save preset — store a filter/sort combination to reuse later.
  • Select rows → push to portfolio — tick companies and send them straight into a new portfolio draft.
  • CSV — export the current result set.

7 · Watchlists

Under Watchlists, create named lists and add tickers (there is a "watch" control on each stock page). A watchlist shows mini price charts and aggregated news for its members — a lightweight way to monitor names you are researching before they enter a portfolio.

Price alerts (buy / sell levels)

Each watchlist has a Price alerts panel. Set a buy level (notify when the price falls to or below it), a sell level (notify when it rises to or above it), or both — the sell level must be above the buy level. You can also click the bell icon on any instrument row to pre-fill an alert for that ticker.

  • When a level is reached, a notification appears in the bell (top bar) linking to the stock.
  • An alert shows Armed until it fires, then Triggered. Each side fires once; use the re-arm control to arm it again, or delete it.
  • Alerts are evaluated every few minutes during market hours against the latest available (delayed) price.

8 · Bonds & ETFs (Instruments)

Instruments has Bonds and ETFs tabs: searchable, filterable tables with coupon, maturity, issuer (bonds) and price/day/YTD (ETFs), each exportable to CSV. Click an ETF to open its detail page with a price chart.

9 · Building a portfolio

Go to Portfolios → New portfolio (or arrive here from the screener's "push to portfolio"). In the builder you:

  1. Name the portfolio, set a benchmark (e.g. ASI) and base currency, and add mandate notes (these guide the AI review).
  2. Add holdings by ticker. Choose weight mode (target %) or units mode (share counts), and set an entry price.
  3. Watch the live validation (weights summing to 100%, warnings on concentration) and save.

On save, the platform computes portfolio analytics (weighted ratios, sector allocation) so the model is ready to manage.

The Portfolios page has three tabs: Model Portfolios (your saved books), AI Builder, and Paper Trading.

AI Portfolio Builder

The AI Builder tab constructs a candidate NGX portfolio from a capital amount and a trading horizon.

  1. Enter an amount in ₦ or $ and pick a horizon — Intraday, Weekly, Monthly or Yearly.
  2. Click Build. Acting as a liquidity-first CIO/Head Trader, Claude proposes positions with defined entry, target and stop levels, expected return and rationale, plus desk warnings.
  3. Every price, unit count and fundamental comes from the platform's live NGN Market data — never invented. Share maths and entry prices are reconciled server-side against the live quote.

Save any pick (or all picks) as a paper trade to track it.

Paper trading

The Paper Trading tab is a simulation — no orders are placed. It shows open positions with live unrealised P&L and closed positions with realised results, plus summary tiles (invested, unrealised, realised).

  • Close a position when you would sell — it moves to closed and books realised P&L at the live price.
  • Convert to portfolio — snapshot your open positions into a new units-based model portfolio (same-ticker trades are merged at a share-weighted average entry). Your paper trades stay open.
  • Clear closed positions — delete a single closed row (trash icon) or use Clear all to wipe the closed history (confirmation required). Open positions cannot be deleted — close them first.
Simulation only. Paper trades place no orders and are for internal analysis. Prices are delayed; past performance does not indicate future results; not investment advice.

10 · Managing a portfolio

Open a portfolio and click through to its management view. The masthead shows market value, day change, unrealised and realised P&L, dividends and benchmark, with an NGN/USD toggle. Tabs:

TabPurpose
HoldingsLive positions: units, average cost, current price, market value, day move, unrealised P&L, and weight vs target (drift).
TransactionsThe ledger. Record buys, sells and dividends (holiday-aware date picker). Positions and cost basis are computed from this ledger.
RebalancingCompares current weights to targets and proposes trades to close the gap. Submit for PFM approval from here.
PerformanceTime-weighted return proxy vs benchmark, and contribution by holding.
RiskBeta, alpha, volatility, tracking error, information ratio, Sharpe/Sortino (editable risk-free), historical VaR/CVaR, max drawdown, and a correlation matrix.
AI ReviewClaude-powered optimisation ideas (see next section).
AlertsPrice and ex-dividend alerts for the portfolio's names.

11 · AI Portfolio Review

The AI Review tab is the platform's flagship analytical assistant. It reads your live holdings plus the current NGX opportunity set and proposes concrete optimisation ideas.

  1. Enter the cash available to trade (₦) — your dry powder to deploy.
  2. Click Generate review. Claude analyses concentration, sector tilt, momentum and P&L against your mandate.
  3. Read the output: Portfolio Diagnosis, Ideas to Trim or Exit, Ideas to Add (names from the live opportunity set, sized in ₦), an Illustrative Reallocation Plan that reconciles cash + trim proceeds against the adds, Risks, and a Compliance note.
How it stays honest. The review uses only the figures the platform provides — it never invents prices or fundamentals. Sizing is explicit arithmetic (units × price). It flags single names above ~15% of the book and sizes new adds prudently.
These are ideas, not orders. The AI review is internal decision-support for your judgement. Validate every figure, then route any resulting trades through the Rebalancing tab for PFM/IC approval. Not investment advice.

12 · Rebalancing & the PFM approval workflow

The platform enforces a two-person control on rebalancing:

Analyst

  1. On the Rebalancing tab, review the proposed trades to move current weights toward target.
  2. Click Submit for PFM approval. The proposal enters the approvals queue and is recorded in the audit trail.

PFM

  1. Open the Approvals queue (clipboard icon in the top bar, or the Approvals page).
  2. Review each pending proposal's trades and rationale.
  3. Approve or Reject. The decision is logged and the analyst is notified.
Separation of duties. An analyst cannot approve their own proposal; only PFMs and admins can act on the queue. Every submission and decision is written to the audit trail.

13 · Forecasting

From a stock page click Forecast (or open Forecasting and pick a ticker). The tabs are:

  • AI Price Forecast — a Monte-Carlo ensemble (2,000 simulated paths from fitted drift & volatility) drawn as a probability fan: the historical line, a dashed median (P50) path, and shaded P25–P75 and P10–P90 bands past a "Today" divider. Switch the horizon (3M / 6M / 12M); hover for the full P10→P90 percentile ladder. Bear/base/bull scenario cards and model diagnostics (drift, volatility, trend fit R², walk-forward error) sit alongside.
  • Buy/Sell Signal — a structured verdict (buy / hold / sell) with conviction, bull and bear cases, and a bottom line.
  • Fundamental Scenario — an implied price from EPS × exit-P/E assumptions with a sensitivity grid.
  • Dividend Forecast — a Claude-written outlook plus a projected dividend-per-share path.
  • Macro Context — the naira/USD trajectory, the NGX All-Share regime, and an AI sector momentum panel reading where the stock's sector sits in the rotation.
Illustrative only. Forecasts are illustrative scenarios — not predictions, recommendations or guarantees — and are labelled as such throughout. Higher backtest error / lower R² means wider real-world uncertainty than the bands imply.

14 · Notifications, exports & theme

  • Notifications — the bell shows triggered alerts: portfolio price / percent-move / ex-dividend alerts, and watchlist buy/sell price-level alerts. Use "check now" to evaluate immediately, and "mark all read". Each alert fires once (re-arm or recreate it to fire again).
  • Exports — most tables offer CSV; analysis pages offer PNG chart export and a print-ready PDF one-pager (browser print).
  • Theme — dark by default; toggle to light with the sun/moon icon.

15 · Quick-start workflows

Analyst — daily research to proposal

  1. Open the Dashboard; skim the AI Daily Summary for the market tone.
  2. Use the Screener to shortlist names; save a preset; push interesting ones to a portfolio draft or a watchlist.
  3. Deep-dive candidates on Stocks; extract the latest filings on the Fundamentals tab.
  4. In Portfolios, open your book and run an AI Review with your available cash.
  5. Take the accepted ideas to the Rebalancing tab and Submit for PFM approval.

PFM — oversight & approval

  1. Check the Approvals queue for pending proposals.
  2. Open each portfolio's Risk and Performance tabs to sanity-check the book.
  3. Review the proposed trades and rationale; Approve or Reject.
  4. Use the AI Review yourself to pressure-test an analyst's thesis before deciding.

16 · Compliance reminders

  • The platform is an internal analytical tool — nothing it produces is investment advice or a client recommendation.
  • AI outputs (summaries, filing extractions, portfolio reviews) are decision-support for a professional's judgement. Verify figures against source data.
  • Forecasts are illustrative scenarios, never guarantees. No output implies guaranteed returns.
  • Prices are delayed up to 20 minutes during NGX hours. Past performance does not indicate future results.
  • Rebalancing output requires PFM/IC approval and is never an order.

17 · Administrator functions (reference)

Admins have a settings gear in the top bar opening the Admin page:

  • Users & roles — create staff accounts (Analyst, PFM, Admin), change roles, reset passwords, remove users. The last administrator cannot be removed or demoted.
  • API keys — configure the NGN Market and Claude API keys in-app; they are encrypted at rest and take effect immediately.
  • Quota & audit — API usage against the monthly pool, recent requests, and the full audit trail of analyst actions.
