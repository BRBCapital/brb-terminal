## Category

An **AI-native quantitative platform for developing and executing systematic strategies in frontier and emerging markets** — starting with the Nigerian Exchange (NGX).

## Positioning statement

> The research-to-execution quant engine built for how emerging markets actually behave — AI-driven signal generation, frontier-calibrated risk, and governed automated execution, on one platform.

## The gap we close (why this, why now)

Global quant tooling assumes deep, liquid, low-friction markets. Emerging and frontier markets are the opposite: data is thin and fragmented, liquidity is uneven and shallow, and naïve models break at regime shifts — the **2016 and 2023 Nigerian FX resets** wiped out strategies that didn't price currency risk. BRB is engineered *for* these constraints rather than in spite of them, and pairs a frontier LLM (Fable) with transparent, rules-based risk so the intelligence is native, not bolted on.

## Value proposition

One platform takes a strategy from **idea → signal → risk-sized book → automated (simulated) execution → performance feedback**, with humans on the governance and compliance gates only — purpose-built for NGX microstructure and Nigeria–UK corridor oversight.
